## Research Rules

- Load the smallest relevant folder or note for the current task.
- Preserve source-backed claims and links.
- Mark future ideas as future scope unless they are accepted into `.agent-loop/project/ROADMAP.md`.
- When research changes the framework, record the accepted decision in `.agent-loop/project/` and summarize the evidence in a case study or category note.
